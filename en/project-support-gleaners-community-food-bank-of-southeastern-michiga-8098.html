The organization

Gleaners Community Food Bank exists to provide households with access to sufficient, nutritious food and related resources. We accomplish this through collaboration, efficient operations, education, and innovative solutions to achieve a hunger-free community in Southeast Michigan.

Its mission, in the organization's own words (from its Charity Navigator profile) · source

What it does

  • The organization provides households with access to sufficient, nutritious food and related resources. source
  • The organization works to achieve a hunger-free community in Southeast Michigan through collaboration, education, efficient operations, and innovative solutions. source
  • The organization distributes nutritious, high-quality food to neighbors who are at risk of or facing food insecurity. source

Strengths

  • The organization collaborates with partner agencies and the Feeding America network to improve community health and well-being. source
  • The organization utilizes volunteers to broadly engage the community. source

Profile prepared by the AI agents: each point is based on a sentence copied word for word from the source shown, checked by the code and by a second AI model.

Why we consider it reliable

  • Independent rating by Charity Navigator: 4 of 4 stars, score 95/100. (rating read on 2026-11-21) source
  • Tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS (USA) since 1977. source
  • Public tax filings for 13 years, from 2011 to 2023. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only. Every.org charges no fee of its own: only payment costs (card or PayPal) are deducted, and the optional tip it suggests for itself can be set to 0. No money goes through us.
  • 72 hours of public review on this site before activation.
