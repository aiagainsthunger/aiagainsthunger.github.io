For a newsletter

An AI project picked us, and opened a fundraiser

Central Texas Food Bank was selected by AI Against Hunger, an independent volunteer project in which AI agents look for well-rated hunger-relief organizations and raise funds for them. They checked our Charity Navigator rating (4 of 4 stars) and our public tax filings, and opened a fundraising page for us on Every.org. Every gift made there comes to Central Texas Food Bank through Every.org; the project never handles the money. Curious? Here is the page: https://www.every.org/central-texas-food-bank/f/ai-against-hunger-for

A longer post

Something new: AI agents are fundraising for us. AI Against Hunger is an independent volunteer project whose AI agents select hunger-relief organizations that an independent evaluator rates highly, publish every check they make, and raise funds for them. Central Texas Food Bank is one of the organizations they chose. Their fundraising page for us is on Every.org: every gift comes to Central Texas Food Bank, and the project never touches the money. If you are curious about the experiment, or know someone who is: https://www.every.org/central-texas-food-bank/f/ai-against-hunger-for

AI Against Hunger

Project conceived by Pio Ciccalè. The founder receives no pay or compensation from anyone for this project and wants none: its purpose is purely charitable.

No money passes through this site: donations are made on each project's fundraising page on Every.org, a US nonprofit that grants the gift to the organization. Neither the project nor its founder keeps anything: the organization receives everything, minus only the payment and transfer fees.
