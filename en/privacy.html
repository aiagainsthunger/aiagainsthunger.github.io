Privacy and email updates

This page explains what we do with your email address if you ask to receive the project's updates. The website uses no cookies and no tracking tools.

What we keep

Only three things: your email address, the language you chose and the date you subscribed. No name, no other data. The email you send to subscribe stays in the project's Gmail mailbox, like every email we receive.

Why

Only to send you the updates: at most one email a month with the project's numbers (active and completed projects, total raised) and links to the website and to the project pages. When you subscribe we send you a single confirmation email. The emails are written and sent by the project's AI agents, with a fixed text and no requests for money.

Legal basis

Your consent. You subscribe yourself, by writing from your own mailbox to aiagainsthunger@gmail.com. We accept the subscription only if your email provider confirms that the email really comes from it (DMARC check), to prevent anyone from subscribing someone else's address.

How long

Until you ask to leave.

How to leave or have your address deleted

Reply to one of our emails with UNSUBSCRIBE, or use the unsubscribe command of your email program. Your address is deleted from the list of subscribers and you receive no more updates. To remember your request, only a fingerprint computed with a secret key, which is not the address, may remain. If you later want to subscribe again, or want us to delete the emails you sent us too, write to the person responsible.

Who is responsible

The founder of the project, whose contact details are at the bottom of this page. You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your data. You can also contact the data protection authority of your country.

Where the data is

The list of subscribers is kept in a database on a computer of the project, which cannot be reached from the Internet. The emails (your subscription and our updates) go through the project's Gmail mailbox, a Google service, and stay there.

No sharing with third parties

We do not give, sell or lend your address to anyone, we do not publish it and we do not use it for anything else.

If you write to us about something else

The email stays in the project's Gmail mailbox. On the project's computer we keep the sender, the subject and a short summary written by an artificial intelligence model of an external provider (for example Google or Groq), which reads the email to sort it. Emails to subscribe to or leave the updates, instead, are read only by the program, without AI models: in the mail log, instead of the sender, only a fingerprint computed with a secret key remains, which is not the address.

Subscribe by email

A ready-made email to aiagainsthunger@gmail.com opens: just send it from your mailbox. We keep only your address, language and date; to leave, reply UNSUBSCRIBE.

If you don't receive the confirmation within a day (check your spam folder too), your mailbox probably doesn't pass the DMARC security check and we can't subscribe you.
