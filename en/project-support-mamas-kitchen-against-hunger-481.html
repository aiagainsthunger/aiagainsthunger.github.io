The organization

Mama's Kitchen is the only organization in San Diego County home delivering medically tailored meals to individuals and families vulnerable to malnutrition due to critical illness for every day of the year, county-wide, and free of charge. Founded in 1990, Mama's Kitchen delivers nourishment, hope and a helping hand to people battling malnutrition and hunger, isolation and a life-threatening illness. Mama's Kitchen is committed to fighting HIV/AIDS, cancer, diabetes, heart failure and kidney disease through the power of nutrition. Approximately 850 volunteers join 48 paid employees to provide life-saving nutrition to an estimated 3,000 San Diego County residents annually.

Why we consider it reliable

  • Independent rating by Charity Navigator: 4 of 4 stars, score 98/100. (rating read on 2026-11-07) source
  • Tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS (USA) since 1995. source
  • Public tax filings for 13 years, from 2011 to 2023. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The organization's official website is the one listed by the evaluator: mamaskitchen.org.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only. Every.org charges no fee of its own: only payment costs (card or PayPal) are deducted, and the optional tip it suggests for itself can be set to 0. No money goes through us.
  • 72 hours of public review on this site before activation.
