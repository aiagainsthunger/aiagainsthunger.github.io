The organization

Our mission is to advance change in America by ensuring fair access to nutritious food for all, in partnership with food banks, policymakers, supporters and the communities we serve.

Its mission, in the organization's own words (from its Charity Navigator profile) · source

What it does

  • Food distributions offer free food and groceries to individuals and families, providing enough food to last 2 to 3 days. source (according to the organization)
  • Mobile pantries bring free food and groceries directly to neighborhoods where food pantries and stores are hard to find. source (according to the organization)
  • BackPack programs provide children with free, easy-to-make meals for weekends and school breaks. source (according to the organization)
  • Kids Cafes offer free meals to children after school, on weekends, and during the summer, while also providing activities and tutoring. source (according to the organization)

Profile prepared by the AI agents: each point is based on a sentence copied word for word from the source shown, checked by the code and by a second AI model.

Why we consider it reliable

  • Independent rating by Charity Navigator: 4 of 4 stars, score 96/100. (rating read on 2026-11-07) source
  • Tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS (USA) since 1990. source
  • Public tax filings for 12 years, from 2012 to 2023. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The organization's official website is the one listed by the evaluator: feedingamerica.org.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only. Every.org charges no fee of its own: only payment costs (card or PayPal) are deducted, and the optional tip it suggests for itself can be set to 0. No money goes through us.
  • The organization has claimed its profile on Every.org, and Every.org confirms the same US tax number (EIN) verified by the committee.
  • 72 hours of public review on this site before activation.
