Support Food Share Of Lincoln County against hunger

The organization

TO ALLEVIATE HUNGER IN LINCOLN COUNTY BY SOLICITING, COLLECTING, AND DISTRIBUTING FOOD THROUGH A NETWORK OF AGENCIES. TO PROVIDE TEMPORARY SERVICES TO INDIVIDUALS AND FAMILIES IN TIME OF NEED. TO ASSIST CLIENTS TO BECOME MORE SELF-SUFFICIENT AND INCREASE COMMUNITY AWARENESS OF HUNGER.

Its mission, in the organization's own words (from its Charity Navigator profile) · source

What it does

  • The organization distributes emergency food boxes through a network of seven affiliated pantries across Lincoln County. source (according to the organization)
  • The charity distributes over 1,000,000 pounds of food, equating to 4,000,000 meals, each year from various supply sources. source (according to the organization)
  • The entity provides food and donated items to local partner agencies to help extend their operational budgets. source (according to the organization)
  • The organization collects and distributes food through agency networks while providing temporary assistance to individuals and families. source

Strengths

  • The organization has been leading community hunger elimination efforts in Lincoln County since 1982. source (according to the organization)
  • The organization maintains public access to its annual IRS Tax Form 990 on its website. source (according to the organization)
  • The charity provides public access to its annual report for Fiscal Year 2023. source (according to the organization)

Profile prepared by the AI agents: each point is based on a sentence copied word for word from the source shown, checked by the code and by a second AI model.

Why we consider it reliable

  • Independent rating by Charity Navigator: 4 of 4 stars, score 97/100. (rating read on 2026-11-08) source
  • Tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS (USA) since 1982. source
  • Public tax filings for 13 years, from 2011 to 2023. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The organization's official website is the one listed by the evaluator: foodsharelc.org.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only. Every.org charges no fee of its own: only payment costs (card or PayPal) are deducted, and the optional tip it suggests for itself can be set to 0. No money goes through us.
  • 72 hours of public review on this site before activation.
