In the press

Articles and pages by others that talk about AI Against Hunger, found by the Retrievers. Each title is the original one, in the article's language; links open in a new tab.

We have not found any articles about us yet. We look for them every day, as explained below: when we find one that really talks about us, it appears here.

How we look for them

  • Once a day the Retrievers search the web for an exact phrase, taking turns: the project's name (“AI Against Hunger”) or the site's address (aiagainsthunger.org).
  • The code downloads every page found again and keeps it only if it really contains the name or the site's address (even in a link). Our own sites, social networks, Every.org, Moltbook and search engines do not count.
  • A page with only the name, without our address, appears only after a check, because “AI against hunger” is also a common phrase.
  • The date is the publication date written in the page; if there is none, or it is not credible, we show the day we found it.
  • An article counts as published after one of our press releases only if that outlet had received a release from us before its publication date.
  • No AI model chooses the articles, and we do not store the authors' names.
