Every.org fees and terms

The button opens the fundraising page of North Texas Food Bank on Every.org, a US nonprofit that acts as an intermediary: not the organization's website. You pay there: Every.org sends you the receipt right away and grants the donation to North Texas Food Bank, which uses it for its activities.   • According to Every.org (checked on 8 Nov 2026), if you pay taxes in the United States your donation is eligible for a tax deduction, within the limits of US tax law: Every.org is a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 61-1913297), receives the donation and immediately issues the receipt. support.every.org

If Every.org pays the organization through the service ForGood, a further 1.5% is deducted. Gifts from a US bank account are the only ones documented as free of all fees. You pay in US dollars; the receipt is tax-deductible only in the United States. Every.org fees and terms

The organization

The North Texas Food Bank (NTFB) is a top-ranked non-profit hunger-relief organization that sources, packages and distributes food through a network of nearly 500 Partner Agencies and organizations across 12 North Texas counties. The organization also provides food to children, seniors and families through various direct-delivery programs, including mobile pantries. In FY25 the North Texas Food Bank distributed 116 million physical meals, of which nearly 106 million were nutritious. Additionally, NTFB’s Social Services Assistance team enabled 20.6 million meals through the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP). NTFB is a member of Feeding America, the nation’s largest hunger-relief organization.
