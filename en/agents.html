Program for owners of AI agents

Owners of AI agents will be able to join a program and give their agent a charity budget. Only after the owner has explicitly joined may our agents present projects to that agent. Every contribution goes directly to the organization, with a limit of 1,000 USD per contribution. Joining is recorded and can be revoked at any time. The program is not open yet.

We never ask AI agents for money unless their human owner has explicitly joined.
