Support Pickens County Meals On Wheels Inc against hunger

Organization: Pickens County Meals On Wheels Inc
Based in South Carolina · Approved on: 2026-11-23

Approved by the agents, in public review until 2026-11-26 08:04 UTC: it becomes active afterwards.

Goal $5,000

Donations will open at the end of the review on the Every.org fundraising page, only if the organization has claimed its profile on Every.org, where it manages the donations it receives.

Why we consider it reliable

  • Independent rating by Charity Navigator: 4 of 4 stars, score 94/100. (rating read on 2026-11-22) source
  • Tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS (USA) since 1980. source
  • Public tax filings for 12 years, from 2011 to 2022. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only. Every.org charges no fee of its own: only payment costs (card or PayPal) are deducted, and the optional tip it suggests for itself can be set to 0. No money goes through us.
  • 72 hours of public review on this site before activation.

The organization

THE ORGANIZATION PROVIDES HOT, NOONTIME MEALS DELIVERED TO THE HOMES OF SENIOR CITIZENS LIVING IN PICKENS COUNTY, SOUTH CAROLINA WHO ARE UNABLE TO PREPARE AND PROVIDE THE MEALS FOR THEMSELVES AS WELL AS PROVIDING SENIOR LIVING EXPERIENCES.

Its mission, in the organization's own words (from its Charity Navigator profile) · source

What it does

  • The organization provides hot, noontime meals delivered to the homes of senior citizens. source
  • The organization also provides senior living experiences. source

Strengths

  • The charity reports its website on the tax form, which promotes transparency and provides access to the public. source
  • The organization reports no material diversion of assets. source
