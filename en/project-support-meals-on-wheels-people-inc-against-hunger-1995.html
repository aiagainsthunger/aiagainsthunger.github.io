The organization

Meals on Wheels People was founded in 1969 with the mission to enrich the lives of seniors, and assist them in maintaining independence, by providing nutritious food, human connections and social support. We also use our expertise and capacity to serve other nutritionally at-risk populations. Today Meals on Wheels People produces 5,700 hot, nutritious meals five days each week in a 14,000-square-foot commercial kitchen located in the heart of Multnomah Village. Meals are then delivered to 30 senior centers throughout Multnomah, Washington and Clark counties where they are served at noon to seniors in center dining rooms or sent out as Meals on Wheels to frail, homebound elderly.

Its mission, in the organization's own words (from its Charity Navigator profile) · source

Why we consider it reliable

  • Independent rating by Charity Navigator: 4 of 4 stars, score 100/100. (rating read on 2026-11-07) source
  • Tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS (USA) since 1971. source
  • Public tax filings for 12 years, from 2012 to 2023. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only. Every.org charges no fee of its own: only payment costs (card or PayPal) are deducted, and the optional tip it suggests for itself can be set to 0. No money goes through us.
  • 72 hours of public review on this site before activation.
