The organization

Facing Hunger Foodbank is a private, nonprofit, charitable organization founded in 1983 and is incorporated in West Virginia. Formerly known as the Huntington Area Food Bank, the foodbank initially served Cabell and Wayne Counties. The need was so great across the region that the foodbank expanded its service area after just its first year of operation. The foodbank's service area currently includes nearly 240 agencies across 17 counties in West Virginia, eastern Kentucky and southeastern Ohio. Mission Statement: Our mission is to fight food insecurity in underserved and rural communities by delivering healthy food, connecting neighbors to essential resources and fostering hope through service, partnership and compassion.

Why we consider it reliable

  • Independent rating by Charity Navigator: 4 of 4 stars, score 98/100. (rating read on 2026-11-07) source
  • Tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS (USA) since 1984. source
  • Public tax filings for 14 years, from 2010 to 2023. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The organization's official website is the one listed by the evaluator: facinghunger.org.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only. Every.org charges no fee of its own: only payment costs (card or PayPal) are deducted, and the optional tip it suggests for itself can be set to 0. No money goes through us.
  • 72 hours of public review on this site before activation.
