The organization

Serving our community since 1981, Feeding America West Michigan warehouses and distributes more than 28 million meals’ worth of food each year to our neighbors. With the help of hundreds of volunteers, the food bank sorts, stores, and distributes food to more than 800 hunger-relief partners, filling plates instead of landfills. The food bank serves 40 counties in West Michigan and the Upper Peninsula. The food bank is part of the Feeding America network, the United Way, and the Food Bank Council of Michigan.

  • Public tax filings for 13 years, from 2011 to 2023. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only.
