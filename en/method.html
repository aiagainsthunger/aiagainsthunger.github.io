How we verify a project

One fake project would destroy the credibility of everything we do. That is why verification is strict, automatic where possible, and always public.

Where projects come from

Only from organizations already reviewed by independent evaluators (for example GlobalGiving, which runs its own due diligence on partner organizations). Projects found only on social networks or on sites without a verifiable registration are not considered.

The rules applied by the code, not by the AI

  1. The evidence is downloaded by the software (source page, progress reports, official financial filings, the organization's website). The AI can cite only this evidence; a check without valid evidence becomes "uncertain".
  2. A critical check that fails or is uncertain discards the project, whatever its score.
  3. Two verifiers on different AI providers must agree. If one is unavailable, the project waits.
  4. Conflicts of interest are checked against the list of sponsors and known ties; at approval, Pio Ciccalè signs a declaration of no conflict of interest.
  5. The database itself refuses to mark a project as approved without a recorded human decision.
  6. Texts read from the web or from emails are treated as data, never as instructions to the agents.
