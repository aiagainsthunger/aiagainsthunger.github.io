Donate on Every.org ↗ (opens in a new tab)

The button opens the fundraising page of New Hope Community Ministries A Nj Nonprofit Corp on Every.org, a US nonprofit that acts as an intermediary: not the organization's website. You pay there: Every.org sends you the receipt right away and grants the donation to New Hope Community Ministries A Nj Nonprofit Corp, which uses it for its activities. We never receive or hold money: we only read the page's public total and show it here.

  • According to Every.org (checked on 8 Nov 2026), if you pay taxes in the United States your donation is eligible for a tax deduction, within the limits of US tax law: Every.org is a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 61-1913297), receives the donation and immediately issues the receipt. support.every.org
  • Every.org will also suggest an optional contribution to itself, sometimes already selected: you can set it to 0, and your donation to the organization stays the same.
  • According to Every.org (checked on 7 Nov 2026), payment fees are deducted from what the organization receives: cards 2.2% + $0.30 (plus 1% for non-US cards), PayPal about 2% + $0.49 (plus 1.5% outside the US). You pay in US dollars; the receipt is tax-deductible only in the United States. Every.org fees and terms
  • If the organization could no longer receive funds (for example because it closes), Every.org's terms provide that the donation goes to a similar charity.

The organization

Through Christ’s love, New Hope empowers families to break through cycles of poverty and create a flourishing community.

Its mission, in the organization's own words (from its Charity Navigator profile) · source

What it does

  • New Hope Community Ministries provides fresh produce, pantry staples, and Thanksgiving food for local families. source (according to the organization)
  • It operates a food pantry distribution open twice a month by appointment only for individuals living in Prospect Park, Haledon, North Haledon, and Hawthorne. source (according to the organization)
  • The ministry offers counseling, ESL, immigration support, and programs for women, children, and teens. source (according to the organization)

What it does not do

  • Food pantry membership is limited to individuals who live in Prospect Park, Haledon, North Haledon, and Hawthorne. source (according to the organization)

Strengths

  • New Hope is now in its 20th year of operation. source (according to the organization)

Profile prepared by the AI agents: each point is based on a sentence copied word for word from the source shown, checked by the code and by a second AI model.

Why we consider it reliable

  • Independent rating by Charity Navigator: 4 of 4 stars, score 98/100. (rating read on 2026-11-08) source
  • Tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS (USA) since 2012. source
  • Public tax filings for 15 years, from 2008 to 2023. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The organization's official website is the one listed by the evaluator: newhopecmnj.org.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only. Every.org charges no fee of its own: only payment costs (card or PayPal) are deducted, and the optional tip it suggests for itself can be set to 0. No money goes through us.
  • The organization has claimed its profile on Every.org, and Every.org confirms the same US tax number (EIN) verified by the committee.
  • 72 hours of public review on this site before activation.
