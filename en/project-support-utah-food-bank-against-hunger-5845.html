The organization

Utah Food Bank remains steadfast in our mission, Fighting Hunger Statewide, by working alongside community partners and volunteers to ensure that our most vulnerable neighbors have reliable access to emergency food assistance. Through a statewide network of more than 380 partner agencies and food pantries, we help close critical gaps in food access by delivering bulk food to partners free of membership or transportation fees. By removing these financial barriers, Utah Food Bank ensures pantry shelves stay stocked so that partners can focus fully on serving Utahns facing hunger.

Its mission, in the organization's own words (from its Charity Navigator profile) · source

What it does

  • The organization distributed 57.8 million meals throughout Utah last year across seven owned and operated facilities. source (according to the organization)
  • The organization distributed food via a statewide network of 390 partner agencies located across all 29 counties. source (according to the organization)
  • The organization distributes food through direct service programs and partner agencies from four warehouses located in Utah. source (according to the organization)

Strengths

  • Founded in 1904, the organization has operated with the mission of fighting hunger statewide. source (according to the organization)
  • The organization shares key documents publicly in the interest of transparency and accountability. source (according to the organization)

Profile prepared by the AI agents: each point is based on a sentence copied word for word from the source shown, checked by the code and by a second AI model.

Why we consider it reliable

  • Independent rating by Charity Navigator: 4 of 4 stars, score 96/100. (rating read on 2026-11-13) source
  • Tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS (USA) since 1959. source
  • Public tax filings for 13 years, from 2011 to 2023. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The organization's official website is the one listed by the evaluator: utahfoodbank.org.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only. Every.org charges no fee of its own: only payment costs (card or PayPal) are deducted, and the optional tip it suggests for itself can be set to 0. No money goes through us.
  • 72 hours of public review on this site before activation.
