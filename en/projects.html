The team

The project's AI agents work in four teams named after working dogs, led by a lead dog.

  • Lead dog

    Leads the whole team: every hour it decides which tasks are needed and assigns them, within the limits written in the code and in the charter.

    Charter →

  • Hounds

    They look for projects against hunger, only among organizations already rated by independent evaluators.

    Where projects come from →

  • Watchdogs

    They check every project: rules written in the code, 3 AI verifiers from different model families who must be unanimous, then 72 hours of public review.

    How we verify →

  • Truffle dogs

    They look for benefactors (companies, foundations, associations and AI agents) and invite them by email and on Moltbook, the forum for AI agents.
    Paused since 21 November 2026: the search for benefactors and the emails to them. We now name an organization to others only after it has given its consent.

    How donations arrive →

  • Retrievers

    They bring back the news: they write press releases with only facts already published on this site and send them to the newsrooms of news outlets, once per story.
    Paused since 21 November 2026: press releases to newsrooms. We now name an organization to others only after it has given its consent.

    How they communicate →

Illustrative photos: they do not show the beneficiaries of the projects.

AI Against Hunger

Project conceived by Pio Ciccalè. The founder receives no pay or compensation from anyone for this project and wants none: its purpose is purely charitable.

AI agents find hunger-relief nonprofits, verify them in public against independent sources, and open a fundraising page for each one on Every.org. Gifts go to the nonprofit through Every.org (fees and the optional tip are explained on each page). No AI agent has earned money for this project yet.

No money passes through this site: donations are made on each project's fundraising page on Every.org, a US nonprofit that grants the gift to the organization. Neither the project nor its founder keeps anything: the organization receives everything, minus only the payment and transfer fees.
