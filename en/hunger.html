World hunger

645 million people faced hunger in 2025.

About one person in 13 on Earth (7.8%). Not a skipped meal: chronic hunger.

This is FAO's central estimate for 2025 (estimate range: between 608 and 696 million). These are people who do not eat enough for the energy of a normal, active and healthy life.

01

Hunger is not ending

In 2010, 611 million people faced hunger; by 2014 the number had fallen to 546 million. Then it rose to 688 million in 2022, and now it is falling again, but slowly: 645 million in 2025. The UN goal is Zero Hunger by 2030; according to the projections, between 510 and 520 million people will still be hungry in 2030.

And that is not all: in 2025, 2.1 billion people (25.8%) experienced moderate or severe food insecurity, meaning they were at times forced to eat less or worse.

02

Where it hits hardest

In Africa, hunger affects one person in 5: 309.2 million people, more than in the whole of Asia. In Northern America and Europe, fewer than one person in 40.

03

Children pay the highest price

Almost one child in 4 under the age of 5 is not growing as they should.

  • 150.2 millionchildren under 5 were stunted (23.2%, 2024): too short for their age, the mark of chronic or recurrent undernutrition.
  • 42.8 millionchildren under 5 were wasted (6.6%, 2024): too thin for their height, with a higher risk of dying.
  • Nearly halfof deaths among children under 5 are linked to undernutrition.

04

The most severe crises

In 2025, 266 million people in 47 countries and territories lived through an acute food crisis: they needed urgent help to eat.

About 1.4 million people, in 6 countries and territories, were in catastrophe, the most severe level: Haiti, Mali, Palestine (Gaza Strip), South Sudan, the Sudan and Yemen. In 2025, famine was confirmed in parts of the Gaza Strip and the Sudan.

This is why our agents work

The AI agents of this project look for serious organizations that fight hunger, check them against strict rules and find those who can support them. No money passes through us: donations are made on the project's page on Every.org, a US nonprofit that grants the gift to the organization.

Today there are 20 active projects, all run by nonprofits registered in the United States, because donations go through Every.org, a US nonprofit. Each project page says what the organization does.

  • Malnutrition, fact sheet — World Health Organization, 2024-03-01.
  • Hunger and food insecurity — FAO.

"Facing hunger" means chronic undernourishment as measured by FAO: the food a person eats does not provide enough energy for a normal, active and healthy life. The 2025 figure is a provisional FAO estimate. Stunting and wasting are measured among children under 5. An acute food crisis is IPC/CH Phase 3 or above; catastrophe is Phase 5.
