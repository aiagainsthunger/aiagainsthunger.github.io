Every.org fees and terms

The button opens the fundraising page of Meals On Wheels Of Central Maryland Inc on Every.org, a US nonprofit that acts as an intermediary: not the organization's website. You pay there: Every.org sends you the receipt right away and grants the donation to Meals On Wheels Of Central Maryland Inc, which uses it for its activities. We never receive or hold money: we only read the page's public total and show it here.

  • According to Every.org (checked on 8 Nov 2026), if you pay taxes in the United States your donation is eligible for a tax deduction, within the limits of US tax law: Every.org is a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 61-1913297), receives the donation and immediately issues the receipt. support.every.org
  • Every.org will also suggest an optional contribution to itself, sometimes already selected: you can set it to 0, and your donation to the organization stays the same.
  • According to Every.org (checked on 7 and 22 Nov 2026), payment fees are deducted from what the organization receives: cards 2.2% + $0.30 for Visa and Mastercard or a flat 3.5% for American Express (plus 1% for non-US cards), PayPal about 2% + $0.49 (plus 1.5% outside the US). If Every.org pays the organization through the service ForGood, a further 1.5% is deducted. Gifts from a US bank account are the only ones documented as free of all fees. You pay in US dollars; the receipt is tax-deductible only in the United States. Every.org fees and terms
  • If the organization could no longer receive funds (for example because it closes), Every.org's terms provide that the donation goes to a similar charity.

Why give through this page

  • Same destination: your gift goes to Meals On Wheels Of Central Maryland Inc through Every.org, a US 501(c)(3) nonprofit that sends you the receipt.
  • Counted in public: our agents read this page's total from Every.org and show it here, so anyone can check how much has been given. We never ask for donors' data, and we do not copy it into our records or use it. Every.org may notify whoever created a fundraising page that a gift has arrived, with the donor's name only if the donor chooses to share it: that notification stays in the project's mailbox until a person deletes it.
  • Nobody here takes a share: not us, not the agents, not the person who conceived the project.
  • Prefer to give on the organization's own website? Do it: the gift helps just the same. It simply will not be counted here. https://mealsonwheelsmd.org/

AI Against Hunger

Project conceived by Pio Ciccalè. The founder receives no pay or compensation from anyone for this project and wants none: its purpose is purely charitable.

AI agents find hunger-relief nonprofits, verify them in public against independent sources, and open a fundraising page for each one on Every.org. Gifts go to the nonprofit through Every.org (fees and the optional tip are explained on each page). No AI agent has earned money for this project yet.

No money passes through this site: donations are made on each project's fundraising page on Every.org, a US nonprofit that grants the gift to the organization. Neither the project nor its founder keeps anything: the organization receives everything, minus only the payment and transfer fees.
