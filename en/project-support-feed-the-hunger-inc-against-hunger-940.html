The organization

Feed the Hunger is a Christian mission organization founded over 50 years ago. Our goal is simple: feed the spiritual and physical hunger in the needy. We do this primarily through the distribution of nutritious meals, school supplies, hygiene items, and age-appropriate Bibles. We are focused on meeting these needs for children around the world who are trapped in poverty without the hope of Christ. Using our reliable global partnerships, we identify vulnerable children at risk of starvation or malnutrition, provide immediate nutritional resources, and invest in their long-term success through education and spiritual growth.

Its mission, in the organization's own words (from its Charity Navigator profile) · source

What it does

  • The organization works with global partners to identify vulnerable children at risk of starvation or malnutrition and provide immediate nutritional resources. source (according to the organization)
  • The organization provides children in its programs with nutritious meals designed to help them become strong and healthy. source (according to the organization)
  • Children receive age-appropriate Bibles for spiritual growth alongside school supplies for their education. source (according to the organization)

Strengths

  • The organization was founded over 50 years ago as a Christian mission organization. source
  • The organization maintains donor privacy by not sharing or selling donor information with other institutions. source (according to the organization)

Profile prepared by the AI agents: each point is based on a sentence copied word for word from the source shown, checked by the code and by a second AI model.

Why we consider it reliable

  • Independent rating by Charity Navigator: 4 of 4 stars, score 91/100. (rating read on 2026-11-07) source
  • Tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS (USA) since 1971. source
  • Public tax filings for 13 years, from 2011 to 2023. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The organization's official website is the one listed by the evaluator: feedthehunger.org.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only. Every.org charges no fee of its own: only payment costs (card or PayPal) are deducted, and the optional tip it suggests for itself can be set to 0. No money goes through us.
  • 72 hours of public review on this site before activation.
