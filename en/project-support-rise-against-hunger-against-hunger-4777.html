The organization

Rise Against Hunger helps underserved people worldwide achieve food security and resilience through nutrition, education and economic empowerment, fueled by a global movement of volunteers and partners.

Its mission, in the organization's own words (from its Charity Navigator profile) · source

What it does

  • The organization assists underserved people worldwide to achieve food security and resilience through nutrition, education and economic empowerment. source
  • The organization works with 3,100 smallholder farmers in Malawi to strengthen food and nutrition security by improving production methods, nutrition practices and household income. source (according to the organization)
  • From 2026-2028, the organization aims to serve 16.9 million people affected by hunger through food security, education, nutrition and economic programming. source

Strengths

  • The organization targets raising $89 million by 2028 to support programs that advance food security and long-term resilience. source

Profile prepared by the AI agents: each point is based on a sentence copied word for word from the source shown, checked by the code and by a second AI model.

Why we consider it reliable

  • Independent rating by Charity Navigator: 4 of 4 stars, score 92/100. (rating read on 2026-11-12) source
  • Tax-exempt status recognized by the IRS (USA) since 1998. source
  • Public tax filings for 13 years, from 2011 to 2023. source
  • 18 checks passed by 3 independent AI verifiers, all in agreement: details in the dossier below.
  • The organization's official website is the one listed by the evaluator: riseagainsthunger.org.
  • The Donate button does not lead to the organization's website but to its fundraising page on Every.org (a US nonprofit), which passes the donation on to that organization only. Every.org charges no fee of its own: only payment costs (card or PayPal) are deducted, and the optional tip it suggests for itself can be set to 0. No money goes through us.
  • 72 hours of public review on this site before activation.
