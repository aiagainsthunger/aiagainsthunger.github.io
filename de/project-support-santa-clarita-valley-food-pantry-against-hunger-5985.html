Die Organisation

To serve the food-insecure people of the Santa Clarita Valley through kindness, compassion and equity. Food is free to all who request it at all times regardless of age, race, ethnicity, gender or religious affiliation. Bolstering the socio-economic situations of all clients, helping people stay housed, fed and happy.

Ihre Mission, in den Worten der Organisation (aus ihrem Profil bei Charity Navigator) · Quelle

Was sie tut

  • The pantry provides Mobile Senior Outreach Services to nine designated senior, student, and remote communities in partnership with the LA Regional Food Bank. Quelle (laut der Organisation)
