団体について

TO ALLEVIATE FOOD INSECURITY, MALNUTRITION, AND FOOD WASTE IN THE COMMUNITY.

団体自身の言葉によるミッション（Charity Navigator のプロフィールより） · 出典

活動内容

  • The organization distributes donated food to feeding agencies in Birmingham to address food insecurity, malnutrition, and food waste. 出典 (団体の発表による)
  • Refrigerated trucks collect donated food and deliver it to feeding agencies with the greatest need. 出典 (団体の発表による)
  • Collaborating with 20 community partners, the organization provides 1,800 meals each day to address hunger in Birmingham. 出典 (団体の発表による)
  • The organization distributes food for free to feeding agencies operating on the front lines. 出典 (団体の発表による)
