关于该机构

The Global FoodBanking Network's mission is to nourish the world’s hungry through uniting and advancing food banks. We were started by a collaboration of food bank networks in the U.S., Mexico, Canada, and Argentina, and the belief that community-led solutions for hunger should be relevant across the globe. Now we’re a Network active in nearly 50 countries. In FY 2023, GFN member food banks recovered over 648M kilos of food and grocery products and re-directed this surplus to approximately 32 million persons facing hunger.
