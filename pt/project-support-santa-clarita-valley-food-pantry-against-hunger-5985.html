A organização

To serve the food-insecure people of the Santa Clarita Valley through kindness, compassion and equity. Food is free to all who request it at all times regardless of age, race, ethnicity, gender or religious affiliation. Bolstering the socio-economic situations of all clients, helping people stay housed, fed and happy.

O que faz

  • It serves families and individuals of all ages who are facing food insecurity and in need of support. fonte (segundo a organização)
  • The pantry provides Mobile Senior Outreach Services to nine designated senior, student, and remote communities in partnership with the LA Regional Food Bank. fonte (segundo a organização)

Pontos fortes
